104 App — Business Model Canvas

Building the first self-service audiovisual production platform — verified crews, 99-minute delivery, and transparent pricing for Canada’s creative economy.

🎬 Value Proposition

Instant booking of verified film and photo crews in 99 minutes. Transparent pricing, automated logistics, and studio-level quality for everyone.

👥 Customer Segments

  • Agencies & brands needing fast creative production
  • SMBs seeking affordable, reliable video marketing
  • Event organizers and festivals
  • Influencers & content creators

🚀 Key Activities

  • Platform operation & development
  • Supplier onboarding & certification
  • Pricing algorithms & routing optimization
  • Marketing, PR, and analytics

🤝 Key Partners

  • TenFour Pictures (Creative & Operations)
  • United Strategists (Tech, Data, Finance)
  • Certified suppliers and film crews
  • Academia for training & certification
  • Gear vendors, insurers, PR partners

🧠 Key Resources

  • Proprietary 104 App platform & algorithms
  • Data room, IP, and pricing framework
  • Supplier and equipment network
  • TenFour’s production expertise

  • API & affiliate integrations
  • Training and certification sales

📈 Cost Structure

  • Platform & app development
  • Marketing & PR campaigns
  • Supplier payouts and operations
  • QA, staff, and compliance
  • Hosting, tools, and automation costs

📢 Channels

  • Affiliate & partner integrations

🧭 Customer Relationships

  • Referral & credit-based incentives
  • CRM & retention automation
  • Community of certified suppliers
